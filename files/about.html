About Me

I build the bridge between public health evidence and public health practice.

I'm a Master of Public Health candidate at Columbia's Mailman School of Public Health, concentrating in Population and Family Health with a certificate in Health Policy & Practice. My work sits at the intersection of program evaluation, health communication, and systems strengthening — designing the logic models, partnerships, and data tools that help public health programs prove, and improve, their impact.

My path started at UC Davis, where I studied Human Development with minors in Public Health and Tech Management and graduated summa cum laude. That combination — human-centered development theory alongside applied technology — still shapes how I approach public health today: I want the tools and the systems to work for the people they're meant to serve, not the other way around.
